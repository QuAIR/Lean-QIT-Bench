Let $\rho_A\in \cS(\cH_A)$ be a density operator on a $d$-dimensional system $A$. Let $\ket{\psi}_{RA}\in \cH_R\otimes \cH_A$ be a purification of $\rho_A$, where $\cH_R\cong \cH_A$. Let $\cE_{A\to B}:\cL(\cH_A)\to \cL(\cH_B)$ be a quantum channel with $\cH_B\cong \cH_A$. Define
$$
\rho_{RB}
=
(\id_R\otimes \cE_{A\to B})
\left(
\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}_{RA}
\right).
$$
The exchange entropy is $S(\rho_{RB})$, and the entanglement fidelity is $F_e=\bra{\psi}\rho_{RB}\ket{\psi}$.
Let $H(t)=-t\log_2 t-(1-t)\log_2(1-t)$ denote the binary entropy.

Let $P=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}$ and $Q=\Id-P$. By considering the binary partition $\{P,Q\}$ on the joint system $RB$, prove the Quantum Fano Inequality
$$
S(\rho_{RB})
\leq
H(F_e)
+
(1-F_e)\log_2(d^2-1).
$$
