Consider the convex combination of bipartite density operators on $\cH_A\otimes \cH_B$ given by $\rho_{AB}=\sum_i p_i\rho_{AB}^i$, where $p_i\geq 0$ and $\sum_i p_i=1$. Let $\rho_A=\Tr_B(\rho_{AB})$ and $\rho_B=\Tr_A(\rho_{AB})$ denote its reduced states.

Define the quantum conditional entropy by $S(A|B)_\rho=S(\rho_{AB})-S(\rho_B)$. The quantum mutual information can then be written as
$$
I(A:B)_\rho
=
S(\rho_A)+S(\rho_B)-S(\rho_{AB})
=
S(\rho_A)-S(A|B)_\rho.
$$

State whether $I(A:B)_\rho$ is convex, concave, or neither as a function of $\rho_{AB}$. Use the concavity of conditional entropy to justify your answer.
