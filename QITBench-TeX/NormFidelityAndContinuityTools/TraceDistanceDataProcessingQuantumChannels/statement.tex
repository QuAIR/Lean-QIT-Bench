Let $\rho,\sigma\in\cS(\cH_A)$ be density operators. Consider the completely positive trace-preserving map $\cN:\cL(\cH_A)\to\cL(\cH_B)$. Define the trace distance by $D(\omega,\tau)=\|\omega-\tau\|_1/2$.
Prove the data-processing inequality
$$
D(\cN(\rho),\cN(\sigma))
\leq
D(\rho,\sigma).
$$
In other words, prove that every quantum channel is a contraction for trace distance on states.
