Let $\rho,\sigma\in\cS(\cH)$ be density operators. Define their trace distance and unsquared fidelity by
$$
D(\rho,\sigma)=\frac{1}{2}\|\rho-\sigma\|_1,
\qquad
F(\rho,\sigma)=\Tr\sqrt{\sqrt{\rho}\,\sigma\sqrt{\rho}}.
$$
Prove the Fuchs--van de Graaf inequalities
$$
1-F(\rho,\sigma)
\leq
D(\rho,\sigma)
\leq
\sqrt{1-F(\rho,\sigma)^2}.
$$
