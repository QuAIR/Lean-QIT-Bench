Let $\rho,\sigma\in \cS(\cH)$ be arbitrary density operators that need not commute, and let $F(\rho,\sigma)$ denote the quantum fidelity. Alberti's theorem states that
$$
F(\rho,\sigma)^2
=
\inf_{P>0}
\Tr(\rho P)\Tr(\sigma P^{-1}),
$$
where the infimum is taken over all invertible positive-definite operators $P$.

For every invertible positive operator $P>0$, prove that
$$
F(\rho,\sigma)^2
\leq
\Tr(\rho P)\Tr(\sigma P^{-1}).
$$
Equivalently,
$$
F(\rho,\sigma)
\leq
\sqrt{\Tr(\rho P)\Tr(\sigma P^{-1})}.
$$
