Consider the pure state $\rho=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}$ and let $\sigma\in \cS(\cH)$ be an arbitrary density operator. Define the trace distance by $D(\rho,\sigma) = \|\rho-\sigma\|_1/2.$ Define the fidelity between the pure state and $\sigma$ by $F(\ket{\psi},\sigma) = \sqrt{\bra{\psi}\sigma\ket{\psi}}.$

Prove the stronger lower bound
$$
1-F(\ket{\psi},\sigma)^2
\leq
D(\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi},\sigma).
$$
