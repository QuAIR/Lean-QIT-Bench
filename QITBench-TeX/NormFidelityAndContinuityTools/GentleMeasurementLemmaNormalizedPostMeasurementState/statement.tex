Let $\rho\in \cS(\cH)$ be a density operator, and let $M$ be a positive operator satisfying $0\leq M\leq \Id.$ Suppose that the measurement outcome corresponding to $M$ occurs with high probability, so that $\Tr(M\rho)\geq 1-\epsilon$, where $\epsilon\in [0,1]$.

Using the canonical square-root measurement instrument, define the normalized post-measurement state by
$$
\rho'
=
\frac{\sqrt{M}\rho\sqrt{M}}{\Tr(M\rho)}.
$$

Using $\|\cdot\|_1$ for the trace norm, prove that
$$
\|\rho'-\rho\|_1
\leq
2\sqrt{\epsilon}+\epsilon.
$$
