Define the bipartite pure state with Schmidt coefficients $\{p(x)\}_{x\in\mathcal{X}}$ by
$$
\lvert \psi\rangle_{AB}=\sum_{x\in\mathcal{X}}\sqrt{p(x)}\lvert x\rangle_A\lvert x\rangle_B.
$$
Its reduced state on $A$ is $\psi_A=\Tr_B(\lvert\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi\rvert_{AB})$, and its entanglement entropy is $E(\psi)=S(\psi_A)=-\sum_{x\in\mathcal{X}}p(x)\log_2p(x).$
Define the standard maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank $M$ by
$$
\lvert \Phi_M\rangle_{AB}=\frac{1}{\sqrt M}\sum_{j=1}^{M}\lvert j\rangle_A\lvert j\rangle_B.
$$
For every rate $0\le R<E(\psi)$, prove that there exists a sequence of separable-operation (SEP) channels $\{\Lambda_n\}_{n\ge1}$ and positive integers $M_n=\lfloor 2^{nR}\rfloor$ satisfying
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}F\!\left(
\Lambda_n\!\left(\lvert \psi\rangle\!\langle \psi\rvert_{AB}^{\otimes n}\right),
\lvert \Phi_{M_n}\rangle\!\langle \Phi_{M_n}\rvert
\right)=1,
$$
where $F(\rho,\sigma)=\left\lVert \sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma\right\rVert_1$ denotes the quantum fidelity.
