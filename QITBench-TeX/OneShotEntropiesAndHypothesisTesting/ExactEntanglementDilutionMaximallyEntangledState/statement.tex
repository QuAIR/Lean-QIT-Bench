Let $\ket{\eta}_{AB}$ be a bipartite pure state with Schmidt decomposition
$$
\ket{\eta}_{AB}
=
\sum_{i=1}^d
\sqrt{\mu_i}
\ket{i}_A\ket{i}_B,
$$
where $\mu_1\geq \mu_2\geq \cdots \geq \mu_d>0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^d \mu_i=1$.
Define the standard maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank $M$ by
$$
\ket{\Phi_M}_{AB}
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{M}}
\sum_{j=1}^M
\ket{j}_A\ket{j}_B.
$$

Using the pure-state separable-operation (SEP) majorization criterion, prove that $\ket{\eta}_{AB}$ can be transformed deterministically into $\ket{\Phi_M}_{AB}$ by a SEP channel if and only if
$$
\mu_1\leq \frac1M.
$$
