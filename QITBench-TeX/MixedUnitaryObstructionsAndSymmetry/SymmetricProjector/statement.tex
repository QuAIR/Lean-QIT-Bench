Let $\cH$ be a $d$-dimensional Hilbert space, and consider $\cH^{\otimes n}$. For each permutation $\pi\in S_n$, let $W^\pi$ be the unitary permutation representation acting on simple tensors by
$$
W^\pi
\bigl(\ket{\psi_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\ket{\psi_n}\bigr)
=
\ket{\psi_{\pi^{-1}(1)}}\otimes\cdots\otimes\ket{\psi_{\pi^{-1}(n)}}.
$$
Let $\Sym^n(\cH)$ denote the symmetric subspace of $\cH^{\otimes n}$.
Define $\Pi_{\Sym^n(\cH)}=(1/n!)\sum_{\pi\in S_n}W^\pi$.
Prove that $\Pi_{\Sym^n(\cH)}$ is an orthogonal projector.
