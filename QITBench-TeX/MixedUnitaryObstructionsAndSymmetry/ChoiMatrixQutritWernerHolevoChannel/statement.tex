Let $\cH=\C^3$. For $X\in\cL(\C^3)$, define $\Phi(X)=\bigl(\Tr(X)\Id-X^T\bigr)/2$. Let $F$ denote the swap operator on $\C^3\otimes\C^3$, so that $F(\ket{x}\otimes\ket{y})=\ket{y}\otimes\ket{x}$.
Let $\Pi_{\ASym^2(\C^3)}$ denote the orthogonal projector onto the antisymmetric subspace $\ASym^2(\C^3)$.

Compute the Choi matrix of $\Phi$ and prove that
$$
J_\Phi=\frac{1}{2}(\Id\otimes\Id-F)=\Pi_{\ASym^2(\C^3)}.
$$
Then conclude that $\Phi$ is completely positive.
