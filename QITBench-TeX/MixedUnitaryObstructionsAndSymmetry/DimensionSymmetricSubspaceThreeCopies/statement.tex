Let $\cH$ be a $d$-dimensional Hilbert space. For each $\pi\in S_3$, let $W^\pi$ denote the unitary operator that permutes the three tensor factors according to $\pi$. Define the three-copy symmetric subspace by
$$
\Sym^3(\cH)
=
\left\{
\ket{\psi}\in\cH^{\otimes3}:
W^\pi\ket{\psi}=\ket{\psi}\text{ for all }\pi\in S_3
\right\}.
$$
Prove that its dimension is
$$
\dim\Sym^3(\cH)
=
\binom{d+2}{3}
=
\frac{d(d+1)(d+2)}{6}.
$$
