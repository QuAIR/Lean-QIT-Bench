With the permutation representation $\{W^\pi\}_{\pi\in S_n}$ from Problem~\ref{prob:qit-symmetry-01}, let $\ASym^n(\cH)$ denote the antisymmetric subspace of $\cH^{\otimes n}$ and let $\sgn(\pi)$ denote the sign of the permutation $\pi$. Define $\Pi_{\ASym^n(\cH)}=(1/n!)\sum_{\pi\in S_n}\sgn(\pi)W^\pi$.
Prove that $\Pi_{\ASym^n(\cH)}$ is an orthogonal projector.
