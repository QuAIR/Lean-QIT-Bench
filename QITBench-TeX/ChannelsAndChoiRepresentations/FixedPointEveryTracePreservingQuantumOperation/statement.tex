Let $\cS(\cH)$ denote the set of density operators on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space $\cH$, and let $\cE:\cS(\cH)\to\cS(\cH)$ be a trace-preserving quantum operation. Using Schauder's fixed point theorem, prove that $\cE$ has a fixed density operator; that is, prove that there exists $\rho^*\in\cS(\cH)$ satisfying
$$
\cE(\rho^*)=\rho^*.
$$
