Let $\cH\cong\C^d$ have computational basis $\{\ket{i}\}_{i=1}^d$. Let $\{\ket{\eta_i}\}_{i=1}^d$ be unit vectors in an auxiliary Hilbert space $\cH_E$, and define the isometry
$$
U:\cH\to\cH\otimes\cH_E,
\qquad
U\ket{i}=\ket{i}\otimes\ket{\eta_i}.
$$
The induced channel is $\cN(\rho)=\Tr_E(U\rho U^\dagger).$
Prove that $\cN$ is a Hadamard channel. In other words, show that there exists a positive semidefinite matrix $H\in\C^{d\times d}$ with $H_{ii}=1$ satisfying
$$
\cN(\rho)=H\circ\rho,
$$
where $\circ$ denotes entrywise Schur multiplication in the computational basis.
