Let $\cH_A$ and $\cH_B$ be finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. Consider the partial trace over $B$, viewed as the linear map $\Tr_B: \cL(\cH_A\otimes\cH_B) \to \cL(\cH_A).$
After fixing an orthonormal basis of $\cH_B$, give an explicit Kraus representation for $\Tr_B$ and use it to prove that the map is completely positive and trace-preserving.
