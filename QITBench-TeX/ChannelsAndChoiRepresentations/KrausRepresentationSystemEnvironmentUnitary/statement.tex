Let $\cH_S\cong\C^2$ and $\cH_E\cong\C^2$. Define
$$
X=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Y=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-i\\
i&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Set
$$
U=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}X\otimes\Id_E
+
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}Y\otimes X_E.
$$
By Problem~\ref{prob:qit-channel-01}, $U$ is unitary. Suppose that the environment is initialized in $\ket{0}_E$. Derive the induced reduced system channel
$$
\cE(\rho_S)
=
\Tr_E\!\left[
U(\rho_S\otimes\ket{0}\bra{0}_E)U^\dagger
\right].
$$
Derive a Kraus representation of $\cE$,
$$
\cE(\rho_S)=\sum_k E_k\rho_S E_k^\dagger.
$$
Identify the Kraus operators as $E_k=\bra{k}_E U\ket{0}_E$, and verify that $\sum_k E_k^\dagger E_k=\Id_S$.
