Let $\cN_{A'\to B}$ be a quantum channel, and let $A\cong A'$. Fix a computational basis and set
$$
\ket{\Gamma}_{AA'}=\sum_i\ket{i}_A\ket{i}_{A'},
\qquad
\Gamma_{AA'}=\ket{\Gamma}\bra{\Gamma}_{AA'}.
$$
The corresponding Choi matrix is $J_{\cN}=(\idmap_A\otimes\cN_{A'\to B})(\Gamma_{AA'}).$
For a state $\rho_A\in\cS(\cH_A)$, define the canonical input state by
$$
\phi_{AA'}=(\rho_A^{1/2}\otimes\Id_{A'})\Gamma_{AA'}(\rho_A^{1/2}\otimes\Id_{A'}).
$$
Prove that applying the channel to this input gives
$$
(\idmap_A\otimes\cN_{A'\to B})(\phi_{AA'})
=
(\rho_A^{1/2}\otimes\Id_B)J_{\cN}(\rho_A^{1/2}\otimes\Id_B).
$$
